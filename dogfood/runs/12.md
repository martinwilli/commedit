**T1-op miss (repeat 2):** the operator judged the branch "already correctly ordered" and skipped the
reorder — `Use format_row in main` stayed parented on `Add parser` instead of `Add util helper
format_row`. This is the **same Sonnet-5 T1 misjudgment** documented in runs 9, 10, and 11 (both trimmed
and shipped operator prompt variants) — reproduces regardless of prompt wording, a model judgment quirk
on this specific cell rather than a regression from the guard added in `8acd0cc9`. That commit's stated
goal was to reduce the *flake rate*, not eliminate it outright, and 2/3 here is in line with runs 9–11's
history on this cell.

**T6-ctl miss (repeat 2):** the skill-less control solver misfiled the working-copy split — it folded
`store.txt`'s remainder into the wrong destination on the first attempt (self-caught via `undo`), then
on the final partition still left `backup.txt` out of `Add backup command` and left `store.txt` sitting
where it didn't belong. This is a **first observation of a T6 miss on `ctl`** (prior runs recorded T6
misses only via T8 session-collision artifacts, not a genuine partition mistake) — worth watching on the
next run, but it's the raw-MCP-without-skill class making the file-partition call unassisted, not a
regression in the operator or its skills (which stayed 3/3 clean on T6 here, consistent with runs 9–11).

**All other 10 tasks × 3 solvers = 30 cells were clean 3/3**, including the tasks most likely to be
touched by the recent doc trims: T9 (`edit_commits` batch identity/re-date), T11 (off-worktree
cross-branch move), and T12 (`blame_squash_targets` routing) all held at 3/3 across every solver.

upward drift continues, but not actionable on its own here.
